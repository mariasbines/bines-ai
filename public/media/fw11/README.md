# FW11 "Thirteen seconds": media assets

Status: installed 2026-10-01. Generated with Gemini / Veo. Fourth take (the first two had a busy, shaded
shelf scene; the third invented a green panel that faded in). Maria picked this one and likes the moon
flipping as it arcs.

- `header.mp4`: 8s silent loop, 1280×720, h264, 24fps. Source was 640×360, 10s; upscaled with lanczos,
  audio stripped. Loop-treated like FW10: source 1–9s, with the final second crossfaded into source 0–1s,
  so the last frame lands on the first.
  A pencil writes on a sheet of paper under a navy night sky; the clock sweeps and the moon and stars arc
  across the sky only while the pencil writes; the pencil is laid down and everything stops.
- `poster.jpg`: source frame at 8.0s. The finished page, pencil down, moon high on the right.

Lessons for next time:
- Write the motion as a **timestamped beat sheet** ("At 1 second... at that exact moment..."). That is what
  finally tied the clock to the pencil.
- State that the background is **fixed from the very first frame**, or Veo invents and fades in panels.
- Veo still delivered 640×360; ask for the highest resolution, or upscale as here (slightly soft).
