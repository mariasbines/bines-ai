## What the image means (the moth exegesis)

The essay's kicker is "Same singularity. Different clothes." — so the hero is the clothes. The dramatic costume of the predicted future hangs unworn (the cords on its sleeves are the wires Maria was wrong about, reduced to decoration). The hanger beside it is empty because the clothes we actually merged in are not in the wardrobe — we are wearing them. The moths are time settling on an unworn prediction: moths move into clothes nobody wears. One moth claims the costume; the other orbits the empty hanger, looking for clothes that left with us. The unworn future's only audience is two moths.

This is the **changed-my-mind** piece's hero — fitting that the artwork changed its mind too, from an impenetrable abstract maze (the old "THE SINGULARITY IS HERE!" register) to a quiet domestic joke.
