## Notes

- The sitemap may not exist as a route — many Next.js sites generate `sitemap.xml` via `app/sitemap.ts` instead, which Next.js handles automatically. Check both patterns.
- If the sitemap is generated by walking content (Fieldwork, postcards), `/gallery` won't be picked up automatically — needs explicit addition.
- Nav wrapping: the existing nav has 8 entries (Fieldwork, Postcards, Changed my mind, Now, Taste, Argue, Archive — that's 7 after Home is hidden). Adding Gallery makes 8. On narrow viewports the nav wraps; verify still graceful.
- This story has no implementation challenges beyond "find the sitemap if it exists." Most of the time is the verification pass.
- This is the last story in the epic. After merge, the gallery is live.
